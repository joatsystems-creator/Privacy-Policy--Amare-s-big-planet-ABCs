Privacy Policy

Amare’s Big Planet: ABCs

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Amare’s Big Planet: ABCs is made for children, and we have built it so that it collects nothing about them at all.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you or your child.

We do not ask for a name, an email address, a date of birth, a photograph or a location. There are no accounts to create and nothing to sign in to.

What stays on your device

The app remembers a few settings so your child does not have to set them again: the age range you chose, whether Calm Mode is on, your sound and subtitle preferences, and a record of which purchases your store account owns, so unlocked letters keep working offline.

All of this is stored on your device only. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Letters A to E are free. The rest of the alphabet can be unlocked with a one-time purchase. Purchases are offered only behind a grown-ups check and are never shown to children.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details. To know what has been unlocked, the app asks the store which items your store account owns, and keeps only the names of those items on your device. We do not receive or keep any order or transaction details. Apple and Google may collect information about the purchase under their own privacy policies.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no social media integration. The only outside components are the app stores’ own purchase systems (Apple’s StoreKit on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play Billing on Android), which are used only when a grown-up opens the store section. We have no servers, so nothing in the app sends information to us.

Internet access

Your child does not need an internet connection to play. A connection is used only when a grown-up buys or restores a purchase, and it goes to Apple or Google, not to us.

Children’s privacy

This app is directed to children. Because we collect no personal information, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google, there is nothing to disclose, share or sell, and no data that could be exposed.

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including provisions for children, and Kenya’s Data Protection Act 2019.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete on request. Removing the app removes everything stored locally.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that collects information, we will update this policy and the date above before that feature ships.
